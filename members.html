Members ✦ The heart of every DUNA

Governed by its members.

A DUNA belongs to the people who join it. Members set the mission, vote on proposals, allocate funds in the treasury, and direct the agents that do the work. Anyone can join from anywhere in the world, members may stay anonymous, and members are legally protected from liability.
