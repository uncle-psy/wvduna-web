Use cases · What the form is good for

What people build with a DUNA

The same legal form behind protocol treasuries and global token raises also fits a Main Street co-op or a county wireless network. A few patterns:

Capital formation

Pool capital fast

Token sales aggregate capital from a global contributor base into a treasury the members control, sometimes in days. The pattern has funded protocols, one-shot asset bids, real-world acquisitions, and civic causes.

DePIN

Run real hardware

Decentralized physical infrastructure networks use token incentives to deploy wireless coverage, storage, GPU compute, or road imagery. Operators contribute equipment; the DUNA handles treasury, governance, and upgrades.

DAO fintech

Govern a protocol

Most large decentralized-finance protocols, from exchanges to lending markets to stablecoin issuers, are governed by member organizations that hold the treasury, set fees, and approve upgrades.

Community & co-ops

Share the upside

Member-governed ventures like the WV Commerce Club let local businesses pool reach and capital and share in the upside rather than being routed around.

Agentic networks

Hold AI agents accountable

Fleets of AI agents that negotiate, transact, and settle need a recognized legal home to sign, settle, and answer for it at law. The DUNA supplies that missing layer.

Service & civic

Serve a mission

Veterans' support, healthcare literacy, agricultural decision support, and civic engagement, run by the people closest to the need rather than a distant head office.
